# PageLens Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 16, 2026**

PageLens is a browser-based page design toolkit. This policy explains what the extension accesses and what happens to it.

## Information PageLens accesses

After you click the PageLens toolbar button, the extension reads information from the current tab as needed for the feature you choose. This can include page text and structure, HTML, computed CSS, the page title and address, image and other asset addresses, and pixels in the visible tab when you request a screenshot. PageLens uses this information to show inspection tools, measure or preview page details, and create exports you request.

## Local processing and downloads

PageLens processes page information in your browser. It does not transmit page content, page addresses, screenshots, or generated notes to a PageLens server. PageLens has no account service, analytics, advertising, or remote storage, and it does not retain page data after the tab session ends. Toolbar position, tool order, and capture defaults are saved in Chrome's local extension storage.

When you choose an export, screenshot, or asset download, PageLens asks Chrome to save the requested file to your device. Clone Page can fetch up to 45 page assets for the ZIP, with per-file and total size limits. The asset collection tool can bundle up to 40 selected files, with a 1.5 MB per-file limit and an 8 MB total limit. The browser requests those files directly from their source hosts; cross-origin restrictions may prevent a file from being included. Those requests are handled by your browser and the asset hosts, not by a PageLens server.

## Sharing and third parties

PageLens does not sell or share page information with the developer. When you open Swap Fonts, the browser retrieves the public font family list from Google's fonts repository on GitHub. When you apply a Google Fonts family, the browser requests the font stylesheet and font files from Google Fonts; that request reveals the selected family to Google and is governed by Google's privacy terms. These requests do not include the page's text or screenshot. Chrome provides the browser APIs used to access the active tab and save downloads. Websites and asset hosts operate under their own privacy policies when your browser requests their pages or files.

## Your choices

PageLens starts only after you click its toolbar button. You can close the tab to end its in-memory session. You control whether to save files and can manage or delete downloaded files in Chrome or your operating system.

## Changes and contact

If PageLens's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are introduced. For questions or support, use the [PageLens GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/xorudra/pagelens/issues).
